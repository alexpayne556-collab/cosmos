## Decision
Weekly: record **what moved that we never saw, and WHY not** — sensor gap? read
gap? conviction gap? The **Friday replay** of the week's 20 biggest winners asks:
what was the earliest public signal, where did it live, what sensor WOULD have
caught it → **Sensor Gap Ticket** → a new Tier-3 sensor task.

## Consequences
This is the primary growth organ of Section 7: **the system expands its own
senses from its own blindness.** Reviewed alongside `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` in the
weekly replay. Loop 4; build Week-2+.
