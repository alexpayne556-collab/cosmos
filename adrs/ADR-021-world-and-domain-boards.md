## Decision
Standing geopolitical **fault lines held as CLASSES, not headlines** — Hormuz,
Taiwan Strait, drone warfare, OPEC, export controls — each a maintained dossier
with a pre-computed **E1/E2/E3 cascade** (direct beneficiaries / suppliers /
adjacents). When it breaks at 3 AM the work is a lookup, not a scramble. Plus
**DOMAIN BOARDS** (technology, gold/PMs, space, bio/medical, energy, defense,
SEC-driven) under the same law (Names Law, ADR-017).

**Reading-List Law:** every domain board carries its upstream trade press. The
domain-press → finance-press **lag IS the purchasable lead time**; provenance
fields measure it (ADR-014).

## Seeds (this build)
`boards/world/HORMUZ.md`, `boards/world/TAIWAN.md`, `boards/domain/GOLD.md`,
`boards/domain/SPACE.md` — populated, Names-Law compliant, write-authority-clean.
Cross-link to Section-5 serial runners (RDW/LUNR/ASTS) and live rows (AMKR, FRO).

## Consequences
A 3 AM break is a lookup. Daily refresh + new fault lines added over time
(Section 7 growth). `board_<domain>` Sheet tabs are the Spark write surface.
